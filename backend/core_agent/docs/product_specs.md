* **Top Notes (Immediate Sensory Volatility / 0-15 Minutes):** Crushed organic black pepper, aromatic organic cardamom pods, and cold-pressed Italian bergamot peel.
* **Heart Notes (Core Scent Identity / 15 Minutes - 4 Hours):** Aged Moroccan cedarwood, dark damp Indonesian patchouli leaves, and night-blooming indigenous Egyptian jasmine.

* **Application Protocols:** Direct the formulation onto high-blood-circulation pulse points, including the interior wrists, the lateral sides of the neck, and behind the ears. 
* **Material Restrictions:** While completely safe for human skin, direct close-range spraying is prohibited on light, delicate fabrics such as natural silk or satin to prevent surface oil spotting.

* **Olfactory Classification:** Warm Ambient Amber Musk (Textile-Safe Room Formulation).
* **Chemical Concentration Architecture:** Formulated with an 8% industrial-grade premium fragrance oil concentration suspended in a completely water-soluble, non-staining fluid base. Engineered for rapid room distribution and long-lasting ambient indoor persistence.

### 2.1 Granular Scent Architecture & Note Breakdown

* **Heart Notes (Core Ambient Identity):** Pure Madagascar vanilla bean extract, crushed Ceylon cinnamon bark, and sticky dark labdanum resin.
* **Base Notes (Ambient Longevity & Dry-Down):** Warm fossilized amber accords, dark aromatic patchouli, and clean, intense white musk fixatives.

* **Strict Material Restrictions:** Do not spray directly onto real natural suede, untreated raw leathers, or highly polished premium wood surfaces.
